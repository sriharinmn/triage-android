Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026. This policy covers the Triage Android app and this website.

The short version

The Triage app collects nothing about you. It talks only to the Sentry server you choose. There is no Triage server, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting in the app. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

The app

  • Your Sentry token is stored on your phone, encrypted with an Android Keystore key that never leaves the phone. It's sent only to the Sentry server you connected (sentry.io or your self-hosted server), in the requests the app makes for you.
  • Issue data (titles, counts, stack traces and the like) goes only between your phone and your Sentry server. Issue lists are cached on the phone so the inbox works offline. Event details aren't saved.
  • Alerts are worked out on your phone by a background check that asks your Sentry server. Notifications are created on the phone; nothing is pushed through a server of ours.
  • Demo mode ("Try with sample data") is entirely on the phone. It never uses the network.
  • Backups: the app's data is excluded from Android's cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer.
  • Deleting your data: Settings → Disconnect removes the token and everything cached. Uninstalling the app removes everything too.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts and images are served from this site itself.

To count visits, this site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. Its script reports the page viewed, the referring site, your country, your browser, operating system and device type, and how fast the page loaded. Cloudflare receives your IP address when the script loads. It doesn't use cookies or local storage to track you, doesn't fingerprint visitors and doesn't follow you across sites. We only see totals. Cloudflare handles this data under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The app itself never contacts Cloudflare.

Children

Triage is a tool for software developers and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The history is public in the repository.
